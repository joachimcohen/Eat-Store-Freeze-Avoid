# Quiz bank (draft)

Here are 12 starter questions, each worked out with the app's own rules and Source data, so the quiz never teaches anything the app wouldn't say. Every food is unopened and stored as the label says unless the question says otherwise. Dates are relative, so the questions never go out of date.

| # | Scenario | Answer | Why (shown after answering) | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Chicken thigh fillets. Use by: yesterday. Looks and smells fine. | Avoid | Use-by is a safety date. Don't eat food past it, even if it looks and smells fine. | Better Health Channel |
| 2 | Sausages. Use by: tomorrow. | Freeze | Freezing before the use-by date keeps meat longer. Use it straight after thawing. | Foodbank Victoria |
| 3 | Sliced ham (prepacked). Use by: in 5 days. | Store | Not at its date yet. Keep it in the fridge at 5°C or colder. | FSANZ |
| 4 | Dry pasta. Best before: 3 months ago. | Eat | Pasta is generally OK for up to a year past its best-before date. | Foodbank Victoria |
| 5 | Greek yoghurt. Best before: 5 days ago. | Eat | Yoghurt is generally OK for up to 2 weeks past best-before. Check it looks and smells right. | Foodbank Victoria |
| 6 | Greek yoghurt. Best before: 3 weeks ago. | Avoid | That's outside the 2-week guidance we use, so we can't say it's OK. | Foodbank Victoria |
| 7 | Sliced bread. Best before: 6 days ago. | Avoid | Fresh bread guidance only goes to 3 days past best-before. | Foodbank Victoria |
| 8 | Cheddar cheese. Best before: 1 month ago. | Eat | Hard cheese is generally OK for up to 2 months past best-before. | Foodbank Victoria |
| 9 | Long-life milk. Best before: 3 weeks ago. | Eat | UHT milk is generally OK for up to 8 weeks past best-before, unopened. | Foodbank Victoria |
| 10 | Eggs. Best before: next week. | Store | Not at its date yet. Keep them in the fridge. | Foodbank Victoria |
| 11 | Baby food pouch. Best before: 2 days ago. | Avoid | Baby food is never shown as OK past its date. | Foodbank Victoria |
| 12 | Tin of tomatoes. Best before: next year. The tin is badly dented and swollen. | Avoid | Damaged packaging can spoil food before its date. | Better Health Channel |
