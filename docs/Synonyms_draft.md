This first draft maps everyday words to the exact Foodbank Victoria category the app will use. Every row starts as Needs check, and none goes live until the project owner marks it OK. The result card always shows the match, for example "cheddar → matched to: Hard cheese".

Rows marked "Not named in the guide" are Claude's best guess at the closest category, so they need the most care.
